# Notes for Section F: Philosophical Support

## User Feedback - Consensus Language

**Issue:** Don't overstate "consensus" on irreducibility of intentionality
**Severity:** Low
**Reason:** While there is broad agreement that intentionality is hard to reduce, notable philosophers still attempt reduction or elimination

**Philosophers attempting reduction/elimination:**
- Daniel Dennett (intentional stance, mild realism)
- Paul and Patricia Churchland (eliminative materialism)
- Ruth Millikan (teleosemantics, biological function)
- Fred Dretske (informational semantics)

**Recommended Language:**
- ✅ "Strong and historically dominant tradition"
- ✅ "Influential line of argument from Brentano through contemporary philosophy of mind"
- ✅ "Substantial philosophical support"
- ❌ "Philosophical consensus"
- ❌ "All philosophers agree"

**Approach for Section F:**
- Present the strong tradition supporting irreducibility (Brentano, Husserl, Searle, Chalmers, Aquinas, etc.)
- Acknowledge reductionist attempts exist (Dennett, Churchlands, etc.)
- Show why reductionist programs face intractable problems (Chinese Room, hard problem, etc.)
- Frame as: "Despite reductionist attempts, the irreducibility thesis has maintained strong support because..."

**Balance:**
- Confidence without overstatement
- Intellectual honesty about debate
- Show why anti-reductionist position is stronger without claiming unanimity
