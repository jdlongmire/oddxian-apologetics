## Work Log

### 1. Hydrotectonic Model Review

**Core Problem Solved:** The heat problem in catastrophic plate tectonics - moving continents thousands of km in months through mantle shear generates lethal surface temperatures (hundreds of Kelvin).

**Solution:** Shift from geothermal to hydraulic driving mechanism.

**Key Elements:**

1. **Pre-Flood Architecture:** Water-saturated lithosphere with distributed liquid water in:
   - Surface basins
   - Crustal aquifers
   - Interconnected fracture networks
   - Overpressured sedimentary sequences

2. **Metastable System:** Hydraulic seals (aquitards) maintained elevated pore pressures until catastrophic breach.

3. **Collapse Mechanics:**
   - Seal failure triggers cascade
   - Pore pressure approaches lithostatic → effective stress collapses
   - Friction drops to negligible (~1% of normal)
   - Blocks hydroplane on thin water films at shallow depths (not deep mantle)

4. **Heat Budget:**
   - Dissipation in water (4x heat capacity of rock) at shallow depths
   - ~7 W/m² flux → ~1 K temperature increase
   - Mantle remains passive - no viscous shear heating

5. **Water Budget:**
   - Earth's total water inventory constant
   - Pre-flood: distributed in crust/surface
   - Post-flood: drained to ocean basins + subducted into transition zone (ringwoodite, wadsleyite hold 1-3 ocean masses)

6. **Transition to Modern Tectonics:**
   - Water network exhausts itself
   - Pore pressures drop → effective stress recovers
   - Blocks re-couple to mantle
   - Slab pull/ridge push reassert control
   - Velocities drop from m/hr to cm/yr

**Testable Predictions:**
- Large-scale detachment horizons
- Chaotic megabreccias at basin boundaries
- Pressure/thermal anomalies
- Geochemical signatures of massive fluid flow
- Mixed fossil assemblages in collapse zones
