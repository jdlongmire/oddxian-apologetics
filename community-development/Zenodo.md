#### 8. About Page (Full Description)
**Value:**
```
ABOUT SCIENTIFIC DESIGNISM

Scientific Designism is a research program systematically examining whether design
is detectable through logical analysis, empirical investigation, and integration of
multiple independent evidence streams.

WHAT IS SCIENTIFIC DESIGNISM?

Scientific Designism investigates the design hypothesis across multiple scientific
domains: physics foundations, catastrophic geology, philosophy of mind, and critical
analysis of naturalistic assumptions. Unlike approaches that invoke supernatural
intervention to bypass physical constraints, all work in this program operates
strictly within natural law while testing whether design-informed frameworks provide
superior explanatory power compared to naturalistic alternatives.

The program is distinguished by:
• Systematic methodology applied consistently across domains
• Integration of three independent evidence streams: historical records (including
  biblical and cross-cultural testimony), physical constraints (natural law without
  exception), and observational data (geological, experimental, theoretical)
• Generation of testable, falsifiable predictions distinguishing design-informed
  models from naturalistic alternatives
• Rigorous quantitative analysis where applicable
• Explicit falsification criteria for all major claims

RESEARCH SCOPE

PHYSICS FOUNDATIONS
Work includes deterministic approaches to quantum mechanics, prescriptive logic
frameworks, logic realism theory, and non-computational ontological models. These
investigations examine whether physical reality requires non-algorithmic foundations
grounded in rational structure.

CATASTROPHIC GEOLOGY
Research develops testable models for rapid large-scale geological reorganization,
including hydrotectonic collapse mechanisms, heat transport through dilatant shear
zones, radiometric disturbance patterns, and hydraulic sorting dynamics. All models
generate quantitative predictions distinguishable from uniformitarian alternatives.

PHILOSOPHY
Investigations span modal logic arguments for necessary being, phenomenology of
consciousness and intentionality, logical priority of intelligence frameworks, and
design inference methodology. Work employs formal logic, mereological analysis, and
systematic evaluation of competing explanatory frameworks.

CRITICAL ANALYSIS
Systematic examination of naturalistic assumptions in evolutionary theory,
cosmological models, materialist philosophy, and uniformitarian methodology.
Analysis identifies untested assumptions, internal tensions, and alternative
frameworks offering potentially superior explanatory power.

METHODOLOGY: THREE EVIDENCE STREAMS

Scientific Designism integrates three independent lines of evidence:

1. HISTORICAL RECORDS
   Cross-cultural testimony (including biblical records) is treated as legitimate
   evidence subject to critical evaluation. This does not assume inerrancy but
   applies the same standards of historical analysis used for other ancient documents.

2. PHYSICAL CONSTRAINTS
   All proposed mechanisms must operate within natural law. No appeals to miracle,
   suspension of physics, or violations of conservation principles. If a mechanism
   cannot work within known physics, it fails.

3. OBSERVATIONAL DATA
   Geological observations, experimental results, and theoretical constraints must
   be explained. Competing frameworks are evaluated on their ability to account for
   the full range of observations, not cherry-picked subsets.

The strength of this methodology lies in consilience: when independent evidence
streams converge on the same conclusion, confidence increases exponentially beyond
what any single stream provides.

EVALUATION CRITERIA

Work is evaluated on:
• Explanatory power: Does it account for more observations than alternatives?
• Internal consistency: Are claims mutually compatible and logically coherent?
• Testable predictions: Does it generate novel predictions distinguishing it from
  competing frameworks?
• Empirical support: Do observations confirm or falsify predictions?
• Falsification criteria: What observations would disprove the model?

Work is NOT evaluated on:
• Conformity to naturalistic assumptions (which themselves require defense)
• Adherence to uniformitarian methodology (when historical records suggest catastrophism)
• Ideological palatability to mainstream consensus
• Prior probability assessments based on current paradigms

PROGRESSIVE RESEARCH PROGRAM

Following Imre Lakatos's framework for evaluating scientific research programmes,
Scientific Designism is assessed on whether it:
• Generates novel predictions not anticipated by competing frameworks
• Expands explanatory scope to new domains
• Leads to new discoveries rather than merely accommodating known observations
• Provides a coherent research direction for future investigation

A research program is progressive if it anticipates new facts; degenerative if it
only accommodates known facts through auxiliary hypotheses added post hoc.

Scientific Designism aims to be progressive by:
• Predicting specific observational signatures (e.g., rapid-slip indicators in
  exhumed shear zones, radiometric disturbance patterns, disequilibrium geochemistry)
• Expanding from philosophical foundations to empirical applications
• Generating quantitative models testable against alternatives
• Identifying new research directions flowing naturally from core principles

RELATIONSHIP TO INTELLIGENT DESIGN

Scientific Designism shares with the Intelligent Design movement a commitment to
design detectability but differs in focus and methodology:
• ID emphasizes biological complexity; Scientific Designism spans physics, geology,
  and philosophy
• ID primarily challenges evolutionary mechanisms; Scientific Designism examines
  broader naturalistic assumptions
• Scientific Designism explicitly integrates historical records as evidence, while
  ID typically brackets theological considerations

The relationship is one of family resemblance rather than identity. Both challenge
methodological naturalism, but through different domains and approaches.

WHO SHOULD CONTRIBUTE

This community welcomes rigorous research from investigators willing to:
• Examine design hypothesis systematically rather than dismissing it a priori
• Generate testable predictions distinguishing design-informed models from
  naturalistic alternatives
• Operate within physical law while challenging naturalistic assumptions
• Subject claims to falsification criteria
• Maintain professional academic standards

Ideological agreement is not required. Methodological rigor is.

CONTACT

Website: https://oddxian.com

Researchers interested in contributing should contact the curator with a brief
description of proposed work and how it aligns with community standards.
```

## Background: Community Naming Evolution

### Names Considered (Chronological)

1. **"The Design Hypothesis Research Program"**
   - Initial suggestion
   - Clean, professional
   - Too narrow for full scope (missing physics foundations, critical analysis)

2. **"Intelligent Design and Scientific Designism Research"**
   - User's first proposal
   - Too closely associated with ID movement
   - Redundant (three design terms)
   - Too long

3. **"Biblical Designism Research Program"**
   - Honest about theological grounding
   - Strong for geology/origins work
   - Seemed odd for pure physics/logic papers (quantum mechanics, LRT/MToE)

4. **"Design and Origins Research"**
   - Broad, professional
   - Too generic
   - Lost distinctive brand

5. **"Scientific Designism: Exploring and Supporting the Design Hypothesis"**
   - User's second proposal
   - Good: distinctive brand, comprehensive scope
   - "Supporting" seemed slightly advocacy-focused

6. **"Scientific Designism: Systematic Examination of the Design Hypothesis"**
   - User's third proposal
   - FINAL CHOICE
   - "Systematic" emphasizes methodology over conclusion
   - "Examination" more defensible than "Supporting"
   - Professional, comprehensive, honest

7. **"Methodological Designism: Systematic Examination of the Design Hypothesis"**
   - Considered as parallel to "methodological naturalism"
   - Rejected: undersells empirical work (70% results, 30% methodology)
   - "Scientific" better encompasses both empirical and philosophical work
