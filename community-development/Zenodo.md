## Background: Community Naming Evolution

### Names Considered (Chronological)

1. **"The Design Hypothesis Research Program"**
   - Initial suggestion
   - Clean, professional
   - Too narrow for full scope (missing physics foundations, critical analysis)

2. **"Intelligent Design and Scientific Designism Research"**
   - User's first proposal
   - Too closely associated with ID movement
   - Redundant (three design terms)
   - Too long

3. **"Biblical Designism Research Program"**
   - Honest about theological grounding
   - Strong for geology/origins work
   - Seemed odd for pure physics/logic papers (quantum mechanics, LRT/MToE)

4. **"Design and Origins Research"**
   - Broad, professional
   - Too generic
   - Lost distinctive brand

5. **"Scientific Designism: Exploring and Supporting the Design Hypothesis"**
   - User's second proposal
   - Good: distinctive brand, comprehensive scope
   - "Supporting" seemed slightly advocacy-focused

6. **"Scientific Designism: Systematic Examination of the Design Hypothesis"**
   - User's third proposal
   - FINAL CHOICE
   - "Systematic" emphasizes methodology over conclusion
   - "Examination" more defensible than "Supporting"
   - Professional, comprehensive, honest

7. **"Methodological Designism: Systematic Examination of the Design Hypothesis"**
   - Considered as parallel to "methodological naturalism"
   - Rejected: undersells empirical work (70% results, 30% methodology)
   - "Scientific" better encompasses both empirical and philosophical work
