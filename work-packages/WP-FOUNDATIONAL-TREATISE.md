## 3. Method: the two tracks

**Track A, exegetical.** Verse-by-verse primary-source exegesis of the load-bearing texts, with competing readings stated and weighed, before any claim built on a text advances past working-draft status (the argument map's standing next gate). Anchor texts: Genesis 1:31; 6:6; 50:20; Deuteronomy 29:29; Romans 8:28-30; Ephesians 1:3-14; Luke 22:42; John 6:38; Hebrews 4:15; 13:20; Revelation 21-22. Original-language work where the argument turns on wording.

**Track B, logical.** Deduction by good and necessary consequence (Westminster Confession of Faith 1.6): every non-explicit claim shows its premises and its inference, in the corpus's axiom, theorem, and lemma form, including formal consistency testing and modal analysis in the manner of the working paper's sections 6.5, 6.13, and 6.20. Observation, established fact, inference, hypothesis, and synthesis are kept distinct.

## 4. Proposed structure

- Prolegomena: authority, method (the two tracks), confessional boundaries (Westminster Confession and Larger Catechism), definitions
- Part I: Theology proper. Eternal triune communion as the moral archetype
- Part II: The eternal covenant and God's good decree. Pactum salutis, election, redemptive purpose
- Part III: Creation. Very good, upright and mutable, imago Dei, relational constitution of humanity
- Part IV: Fall and sin. Closed self-interest as root, tested against counterexamples; providence and secondary causes
- Part V: The decree and the problem of evil. Logical and evidential forms, modal distinctions, the ordained minimum, apparent vs actual gratuitousness, the Deuteronomy 29:29 boundary
- Part VI: Law and accommodation. Revelation of righteousness vs restraint and accommodation; hard texts including Leviticus 25:44-46
- Part VII: Christology. One person, two natures, two wills, impeccability, restored communion
- Part VIII: Consummation. Perfected loving communion: confirmed, incorruptible, glorified freedom without capacity for evil
- Part IX: Ethics and apologetic deployment. Moral order, the slavery case study, the communion argument outward
- Part X: Objections register and open questions, cross-tradition disagreement named honestly
- Appendices: Scripture matrix; unified argument map and epistemic ledger; source verification register
