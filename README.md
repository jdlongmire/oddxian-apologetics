# oddXian Apologetics

**CHALLENGE THE CONSENSUS / Working out the harmony of God's Word and God's World**

A repository for Christian apologetics resources and development, supporting [oddxian.com](https://oddxian.com).

## About

This repository contains research, arguments, and development materials for rigorous Christian apologetics. Rather than accepting mainstream cultural narratives uncritically, this work emphasizes logical argumentation and scriptural fidelity over cultural accommodation.

The goal is to provide intellectually sound, biblically-grounded responses that challenge popular consensus where it conflicts with Christian doctrine—examining the intersections between biblical interpretation and worldly perspectives with philosophical precision.

This work serves as an adjunct to Logic Realism Theory, applying systematic philosophical frameworks to the defense of orthodox Christian faith.

## Mission

- Challenge logically inconsistent arguments against Christianity
- Expose cultural reframing that distorts scriptural meaning
- Develop systematic theological and philosophical frameworks
- Research and articulate classical Christian positions
- Support the oddxian.com publication with substantive content

## Core Focus Areas

### Philosophical Apologetics
- Logical arguments for God's existence
- Burden of proof in atheism debates
- Critique of naturalistic presuppositions
- Epistemological foundations

### Biblical Interpretation
- Scriptural fidelity vs. cultural accommodation
- Confronting progressive reinterpretations
- Hermeneutical principles
- Theological consistency

### Cultural Critique
- Examining emotionalism in modern theology
- Challenging consensus narratives
- Defending traditional Christian ethics against cultural drift
