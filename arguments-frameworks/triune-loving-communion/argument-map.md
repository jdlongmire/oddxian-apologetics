## Dependency structure

P1-P4 support the proposed eternal-communion ontology. P5-P6 ground creaturely moral vocation. P7 explains the Fall and particular sins if its universal scope survives counterexample testing. P8 distinguishes righteous law from historical governance. P9-P10 link reconciliation with covenantal communion. P11-P12 offer explanatory parsimony and do not substitute for divine self-revelation.

## Critical tests

1. Test P7 against culpable ignorance, omission, fear, false belief, misplaced loyalty, and self-sacrificial wrongdoing. Define self-interest independently of the classification of an act as sinful.
2. Test P12 against models of perfect reciprocal love and the distinction between the analytic minimum for triadic relations and the metaphysical necessity of exactly three divine persons.
3. Examine whether the existing slavery manuscript's Noahic-covenant binary is exhaustive; enumerate alternative forms of governance.
4. Assess Leviticus 25:44-46 and Philemon as potential countertexts to any simplistic accommodation-to-abolition inference.
5. Separate claims directly asserted by Scripture from theological constructions such as the *pactum salutis*.
