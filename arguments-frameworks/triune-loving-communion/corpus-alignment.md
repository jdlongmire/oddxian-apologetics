## Integration decisions

Preserve the existing manuscripts. Develop the triune communion framework as an upstream systematic account of why love is foundational. Do not claim that the earlier slavery manuscript already proves Trinitarian ontology. The imago Dei connection to self-love is a theological inference, not an explicit biblical definition.

## Outstanding scrutiny

1. Test the universal claim that every sin derives from closed self-interest against ignorance, omission, fear, and misguided altruism.
2. Distinguish a closed dyad by definition from the assertion that any two-person relationship is obsessive.
3. Test the claim that the Noahic covenant permits only two divine governance options; this is not established by merely citing Genesis 8-9.
4. Treat Leviticus 25:44-46 as a serious countertext and establish independent criteria for accommodation.
5. Distinguish eternal triune life from the systematic inference of a covenant of redemption.
