**Check 11.7: 22:14 plynontes (critical) against poiountes tas entolas (TR); 22:19 xylou against Erasmus's biblou.**
22:14: CONFIRMED. The tsebi.com textual analysis sets the two readings side by side (TR "poiountes tas entolas autou"; Tischendorf "plynontes tas stolas auton") and notes the uncial graphic similarity the study cites from Swete; Vincent's Word Studies directs "Read hoi plynontes tas stolas auton"; a collation summary (brandplucked.com, an adversarial KJV-side source whose factual attestation agrees) records plynontes in Sinaiticus and Alexandrinus, the Latin Vulgate (ms 425), Old Latin ar, Sahidic, and Ethiopic, and quoted by Fulgentius, matching the study's attestation claim drawn from Kelly and Swete.
22:19: mechanism CONFIRMED, quantifier CORRECTED. The NET textual note (quoted in full at textus-receptus.com) states that the TR reads apo biblou, that Erasmus had no Greek mss for the last six verses and translated the Latin Vulgate back into Greek, creating seventeen variants found in no Greek mss, and that "the tree" of life is "found in the best and virtually all Greek mss." Krans (TC: A Journal of Textual Criticism 16, 2011) confirms the retranslation account and the manuscript: minuscule 2814 (formerly 1r), whose last six verses (22:16-21) are lost. But the study's sentence "Every Greek manuscript reads apo tou xylou tes zoes" overstates: the laparola apparatus records biblou in Greek mss 296 and 2049 and in the margin of 2067 (late copies standing in or behind the Latin tradition), alongside its versional and patristic support. ## Summary of required corrections

Two corrections, both in study 11, both precision fixes. Studies 09 and 10 require none.

**Correction 1 (Study 11, section 6, the "22:19" paragraph; factual overstatement).**
The sentence "Every Greek manuscript reads apo tou xylou tes zoes, 'from the tree of life.'" is not correct as quantified: biblou is read by a few late Greek copies (296, 2049, and the margin of 2067, per the laparola apparatus), witnesses standing in or behind the Latin tradition, in addition to its Latin and patristic support. Replace that sentence with:
"Virtually all Greek manuscripts read apo tou xylou tes zoes, 'from the tree of life' (NET: 'the best and virtually all Greek mss'); the rival biblou survives in a few late Greek copies (296, 2049, and the margin of 2067) that stand in or behind the Latin tradition."
The remainder of the paragraph stands as verified: the TR/KJV "book of life" entered through Erasmus, whose single Revelation manuscript (minuscule 2814) lacks the closing verses (22:16-21); he supplied them by translating back from the Latin Vulgate, rendering its libro as biblou. Optional tightening, not required: where the paragraph says the manuscript "lacked its final leaf," the sources specify that the last six verses (22:16-21) are lost.

**Correction 2 (Study 11, section 3.3, the krauge bullet; unqualified distribution).**
Replace "within Revelation the word occurs only here" with "within Revelation, in the critical text, the word occurs only here (the Received Text also reads krauge megale at 14:18)." The study's argument (John abolishes the crying itself, strengthening Isa 65:19) is unaffected, since the study follows the critical text throughout; the qualification is needed because the claim as printed is falsified by the TR at 14:18.
