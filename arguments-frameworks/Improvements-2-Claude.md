- Collectively: Overwhelmingly convincing

**This is exactly the structure of the Consilience Argument:**
- Multiple independent lines of evidence

- ¬H = Naturalism (or other alternatives)
- PSR = Principle of Sufficient Reason holds
- E₁, E₂, E₃, E₄ = Evidence from four demonstrations

P(H | E₁, E₂, E₃, E₄) = 

P(H | E₁, E₂, E₃, E₄, PSR) × P(PSR) + P(H | E₁, E₂, E₃, E₄, ¬PSR) × P(¬PSR)

- All use PSR (or something close)
- Demos I and III both invoke personhood
- Demos II and III both involve rationality

**Account for this:**

P(all four fail) ≠ P(D1 fails) × P(D2 fails) × P(D3 fails) × P(D4 fails)

Instead, use conditional probabilities:

P(all four fail | PSR holds) ≈ 0.01 (very small if PSR granted)
P(all four fail | PSR fails) ≈ 0.5 (higher if PSR rejected)

**Overall:**
P(all four fail) = 0.01 × P(PSR holds) + 0.5 × P(PSR fails)

**If P(PSR) = 0.8:**
P(all four fail) = 0.01 × 0.8 + 0.5 × 0.2 = 0.008 + 0.1 = 0.108

**Therefore:**
P(at least one succeeds) = 1 - 0.108 = **89.2%**

**If P(PSR) = 0.9:**
P(at least one succeeds) ≈ **95%**

**This is honest and rigorous.**
