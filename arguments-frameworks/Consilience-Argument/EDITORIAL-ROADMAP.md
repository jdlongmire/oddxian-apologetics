## Professional Editorial Punch-List

### 1. **Length Reduction: Cut 35-40% (45k → 28-32k words)**

**Problem:** Repetition kills pacing
- Every demonstration re-derives necessity, eternality, simplicity, etc. from scratch
- Objections sections repeat same counter-moves (PSR, brute facts, "God of the gaps")
- Inescapability Thesis + Convergence sections both eliminate naturalism, Platonism, etc.

**Solution:**
- **One unified attribute-derivation table** at end of Convergence section
  - Refer back to it instead of re-deriving in each demonstration
  - Keep demonstration-specific derivations minimal (1-2 sentences max)

- **Merge all repeated objections** into new **Part V: "Global Objections and Rebuttals"**
  - PSR contestation
  - Divine simplicity worries
  - Gödel applicability
  - Brute facts objection
  - "God of the gaps" charge
  - Cross-reference from demonstrations instead of repeating

- **Ruthlessly cut rhetorical flourishes**
  - Search for phrases appearing 5+ times:
    - "This is performatively undeniable"
    - "The dialectical trap"
    - "Consilience power"
  - Keep once, remove repetitions

**Target:** 30k words = "book-chapter dense" but readable in two sittings

---

### 2. **Restructure for Two Distinct Audiences**

**Current Problem:** Tries to be everything to everyone → feels slightly schizophrenic

**Option A: Academic Philosophy Route** (recommended for journal citations)
- **Front-matter:** Short 3-4 page executive summary for popular reader
- **Main text:** Dive straight into four demonstrations
  - Formal syllogisms
  - Numbered premises
  - Minimal rhetoric
- **Move pastoral language** ("for the believer / for the skeptic") to appendix or epilogue

**Option B: Intelligent-Popular Route** (like Swinburne's shorter books, Dougherty & Pruss)
- Keep current warm tone
- **Add clearer signposting:**
  - Summaries every 5-7 pages
  - "Where we are" markers
  - Chapter roadmaps

---

### 3. **Revise Confidence Percentages**

**Problem:** "99.97%" reads like pseudo-precision → triggers academic eye-rolls

**Three Options:**

**A. Qualitative Bayesian Language** (recommended for academic)
- "Posterior probability overwhelmingly high even from skeptical priors"
- "Rational credence approaches certainty"
- "Probability that all four fail is vanishingly small"

**B. Move to Appendix** (recommended for popular)
- Create **Appendix: "A Toy Bayesian Model"**
- Emphasize it is *illustrative*, not dogmatic
- Keep calculation but contextualize limitations

**C. Swinburne-Style Language**
- "Raises probability of theism above 0.97 on most reasonable priors"
- "Cumulative case yields practical certainty"
- "Even halving individual confidences yields >95%"

**Action:** Replace all instances of "99.97%" in main text with chosen option

### 4. **Strengthen Two Weakest Links**

#### A. **Divine Simplicity Defense (2-3 pages)**

**Current Status:** Asserted repeatedly, never defended against modern objections

**Required Engagements:**
1. **Alvin Plantinga's objections:**
   - Sovereignty-aseity complaint
   - Property vs. essence distinction worries

2. **Brower-Rea truthmaker issues:**
   - How can simple God ground diverse truths?
   - Truthmaker principle challenges

3. **Modern analytic concerns:**
   - Divine simplicity vs. divine freedom
   - Simplicity vs. Trinity (acknowledge, defer to revelation)

**Location:** Add subsection in Convergence chapter OR new appendix

**Approach:**
- Thomistic defense (actus purus response)
- Distinguish simplicity from "being featureless"
- Show how simplicity follows from demonstrations, not imposed

---

#### B. **PSR Contestation (1-2 pages)**

**Current Status:** Rebuttals good but thin

**Required Engagements:**
1. **Peter van Inwagen's "mystery" response:**
   - "PSR is less plausible than the mystery it avoids"
   - Response: PSR is performatively presupposed in inquiry

2. **Graham Oppy's symmetry objection:**
   - "Necessary God is just as 'brute' as brute universe"
   - Response: God is self-explanatory (essence = existence), universe is not

3. **Delaunay/Manchak cosmological contingency arguments:**
   - Modern physics challenges to PSR
   - Response: Physical contingency vs. metaphysical necessity distinction

**Location:** Expand PSR section in "Global Objections" (new Part V)

**Approach:**
- Distinguish strong PSR (everything) from weak PSR (contingent things)
- Show demonstrations use weak PSR
- Defend even strong PSR as rational default
