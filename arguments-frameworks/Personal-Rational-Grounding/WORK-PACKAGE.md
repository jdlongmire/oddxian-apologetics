# Work Package: The Necessity of Personal Rational Grounding

Date: 2026-11-28
Owner: James (JD) Longmire
Status: Captured for development and adversarial validation; not a certified metaphysical proof.
Provenance: Owner-led dialogue of 2026-11-28 on worldview grounding, truth, intentionality, and triadic actualization.

## Objective and thesis

Develop a rigorous argument that a non-contingent personal rational source is necessary for an ultimately sufficient, non-arbitrary explanation of contingent, informationally coherent actuality. The owner maintains that impersonal alternatives either presuppose the conditions requiring explanation, eliminate contingency, or terminate in arbitrary determination, contrary to the epistemic endeavor.

Preserve the distinction between explanatory necessity under explicit grounding criteria and unconditional metaphysical necessity. Do not repeatedly reopen settled definitional entailments as independent obligations.

## Definitions

- Objective truth: truth independent of contingent subjective judgment. Necessary truth: truth that cannot be otherwise. Objective contingent truths must be distinguished from necessary truths.
- Worldview grounding: foundational axioms, presuppositions, core principles and epistemic authorities through which truth is apprehended, derived and evaluated. Ontological grounding concerns what makes those conditions obtain; epistemic justification concerns warrant for accepting them.
- Skepticism: critical evaluation through epistemic criteria; external critique evaluates competing claims, internal critique evaluates their consistency with their own commitments, and reflexive critique examines one's own grounding.
- Logic (L3): identity, noncontradiction, excluded middle in the classical actualization framework. Mathematics presupposes logical distinctions and inference; this alone does not establish that every mathematical formalism requires classical logic.
- Information (I∞): unbounded informational potential; determinate states instantiate distinctions. Distinguish semantic knowledge from physical information.
- Action (A0): primitive actualizing efficacy; distinguish fundamental actualization from derivative event causation.
- Intention: rationally directed mental action toward an object or end. Personal agency: a subject capable of knowing, intending and exercising will. Under these definitions intention is personal; no separate personhood proof is needed after intentionality is established.
- Personal reason: rational apprehension and evaluation of alternatives and direction of action for a reason. Distinguish explanatory reasons from necessitating causes.
- Absolute arbitrariness: absence of ordering constraints. Contrastive arbitrariness: absence of sufficient reason for a particular outcome among admissible alternatives. Neither should be conflated with stochastic behavior within an ordered system.
- Ontological priority: dependence, not necessarily temporal precedence.

## Captured argument and status ledger

E1. The categorical denial of objective truth, asserted as objectively true, is self-defeating. At least one objective truth exists. The necessary existence of truth is defended by the impossibility of its categorical denial; distinguish this from necessity of every truth.
E2. Coherent inquiry presupposes logical distinctions and valid inference. Radical skepticism asserted as knowledge that knowledge is impossible defeats itself; suspension of judgment and fallibilism are distinct.
E3. Skepticism is an epistemic instrument dependent upon grounding. Truth is independent of human judgment; apprehension is fallible.
E4. Necessary truth cannot derive its necessity from contingent cognition or action alone. Contingent processes may instantiate or discover necessary truth.
E5. Mathematics presupposes logic. Mathematical descriptions alone do not supply actualizing efficacy.
E6. Physical reality exhibits determinate informational coherence and persistent regularities. This is an observational starting point, not proof that every particular outcome has a contrastive explanation.
E7. TRT working framework: χ ≡ A₀(I∞ | L₃). Logic, information and action are categorically distinct, jointly indispensable co-primitives of determinate actualization. Co-primitivity is a framework commitment requiring independent validation; it does not imply three separate substances or a fourth integration mechanism.
E8. Emergence and derivative physical processes presuppose antecedent generative order, distinctions and efficacy. Emergence cannot explain the ultimate conditions of its own possibility. Ordinary physical action need not itself intend; the owner maintains it depends ultimately on antecedent intentionality.
E9. Abstract propositions and mathematical descriptions, considered solely as abstractions, possess no intrinsic agency. A wholly structureless or illogical source cannot coherently specify determinate state transitions.
E10. Intention is action. Rational intention entails a personal subject. A personal mind unifies rationality, knowledge and intentional efficacy; the owner regards this as the intrinsic unity of the triad, not an additional primitive.
E11. Logic constrains admissible states; information distinguishes possibilities; action actualizes. Where genuine alternatives exist, a particular actualization requires determination. The owner argues fundamental initiation requires decision, unlike derivative propagation of causal chains.
E12. If complete determining conditions are necessary and entail an outcome, that outcome is necessary. This cannot explain genuine metaphysical contingency without qualification.
E13. Stochastic processes presuppose a possibility space and operative constraints. A primitive impersonal stochastic account may stipulate those conditions, but does not by itself provide a sufficient contrastive reason for a particular outcome.
E14. Personal reason offers an account of contingent determination through a subject acting for reasons without necessitating antecedent events, conditional on the coherence of non-necessitating agent causation.
E15. The epistemic endeavor seeks rational explanation. An ontology terminating in brute contingent determination abandons ultimate rational explanation at that point. It may still support local rational inquiry; this is an explanatory failure under the stated criterion rather than an automatic formal contradiction.

## Dialogue development and owner commitments

The discussion began with worldview grounding as axioms and core principles from which truth may be obtained, then skepticism as evaluation through grounding. The impossibility of denying objective truth motivated the question of its source. The transmitter-receiver analogy distinguished source, transmission, reception and interpretation; reception of information does not alone establish intentional transmission. The owner argued that absent intentionality truth is inert, that intention is action, and that contingent non-rational action cannot produce non-contingent truth.

The discussion moved to structurelessness, mathematical dependence on logic, and emergence. Absolute lack of order cannot ground coherent actuality. Impersonal logical conformity must be distinguished from personal rationality until their necessary dependence is demonstrated. The owner emphasized co-primitivity, ontological precedence, the impossibility of impersonal intention, and the role of decision in initiating a causal chain. Personal reason became the proposed basis for non-arbitrary determination among genuine alternatives.

The final exchange sharpened the epistemic critique: physical reality is ordered; absolute arbitrariness cannot supply order; contrastively arbitrary brute determination abandons rational explanation. The owner maintains no sufficient impersonal explanatory alternative is available. Preserve this positive thesis and test it rather than treating mere stipulation of a brute fact as an explanation.

## Formal argument candidate

Let C = genuine contingent fundamental actuality; R = sufficient non-arbitrary rational explanation of its determination; P = personal rational agency; N = non-contingent ultimate ground.

1. C and informational coherence obtain (defend metaphysical contingency separately from empirical variability).
2. Necessary truth and logical constraints cannot be grounded solely in contingent conditions.
3. Ordered actualization presupposes L3, I∞ and A0.
4. Fundamental determination cannot be explained by an antecedent derivative mechanism.
5. Under the proposed exhaustive classification, fundamental determination is necessitated, brute/contrastively arbitrary, or intentional for reasons.
6. C excludes necessitated determination by wholly necessary complete conditions.
7. R excludes brute/contrastively arbitrary determination.
8. Intentional determination entails P by definition.
9. Therefore (C ∧ R) → P, conditional on classification and explanatory adequacy.
10. Given the additional grounding premise that the ultimate source of necessary order must itself be non-contingent and unified with actualizing agency, conclude N ∧ P.

Contraposition: ¬P → (¬C ∨ ¬R), subject to the same premises. This is a valid conditional inference; soundness depends on the premises. The owner proposes the Principle of Fundamental Rational Determination: every fundamental contingent actualization possesses a sufficient explanatory basis for its particular determination without rendering it metaphysically necessary.

## Epistemic asymmetry and explanatory criterion

An impersonal brute-fact account employs rationality epistemologically while accepting arbitrary determination ontologically. It can remain a stipulation but cannot count as an ultimately rationally sufficient explanation merely by naming its primitives. Distinguish unknown reasons from nonexistent reasons. Apply the same standard to the personal account: necessary existence and intrinsic rationality do not automatically explain a particular choice. The account must show how reasons and will supply sufficient explanation without modal collapse or unexplained selection.

## Falsification and acceptance criteria

A coherent impersonal countermodel satisfying necessary logical order, genuine contingency, actualizing efficacy and the same non-arbitrary contrastive explanatory standard defeats the exclusivity claim. A demonstration that the personal account cannot explain contingent choice without necessity or arbitrariness defeats its proposed sufficiency. A failure of the grounding principle narrows the conclusion to conditional explanatory necessity. Mere naming of an unexplained primitive does not satisfy the stated explanatory criterion, but absence of a known alternative alone does not prove metaphysical impossibility.

Acceptance: preserve owner thesis, distinguish definitions/observations/framework commitments/conditional results/open obligations, validate formal inferences and competing models, and obtain owner approval before claiming an unconditional theorem or publishing.
