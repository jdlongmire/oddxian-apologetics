# Literal Programmatic Intervention: A Logos-Grounded Framework for Biblical Creation

**James (JD) Longmire**
Northrop Grumman Fellow (unaffiliated research)

*Author's Note: This paper represents an ongoing refinement of an initial thesis. The framework continues to develop through engagement with scientific literature, theological critique, and collaborative discussion.*

## Abstract

Every origins model operates from unfalsifiable axioms. The debate is not science versus faith but a clash between competing faith commitments about the unobserved past. This paper argues that biblical theism uniquely grounds the preconditions of intelligibility that make scientific inquiry possible, and from that foundation presents Literal Programmatic Intervention (LPI) as a coherent framework for understanding creation.

LPI is neither traditional young-earth nor old-earth creationism but an integrated third way. It maintains that God designed natural systems with latent capacities, then coordinated these systems through direct action on temporal parameters and initial conditions to achieve specific outcomes within literal six-day creation. The framework proposes testable physical mechanisms (hydrotectonic collapse, thermodynamic heat budgets) while acknowledging that certain elements (multi-threaded time architecture) represent miraculous divine coordination rather than natural processes.

Unlike defensive harmonization attempts, LPI proceeds from the recognition that the biblical framework grounds the very intelligibility naturalism presupposes.

## I. The Epistemological Foundation

### 1.1 The Preconditions Problem

All inquiry presupposes conditions it cannot establish from within itself:

- **Uniformity:** Science assumes physical regularities observed in one context hold in others
- **Rational Order:** Inquiry assumes reality has structure accessible to reason
- **The Efficacy of Logic:** Reasoning assumes logical laws are truth-preserving
- **Mind-World Correspondence:** Knowledge assumes cognitive faculties are reliable

These preconditions are not optional. Deny any one and inquiry collapses. Yet they cannot be grounded by the inquiry that depends on them.

### 1.2 The Logos as Ground

Biblical theism provides the necessary grounding.

The universe proceeds from rational agency. The Logos (John 1:1-3) is the rational and ordering principle through whom all things were made. Creation is therefore not brute fact but the product of mind, and its intelligibility is a feature rather than an accident.

- **Uniformity grounded:** Physical regularities hold because they are sustained by consistent rational will
- **Rational order grounded:** The correspondence between mind and reality is explained by common origin
- **Logic grounded:** Logical laws reflect the character of God's thought
- **Mind-world correspondence grounded:** Cognitive faculties are designed by the same agent who designed the world

### 1.3 Positioning: Beyond Young Earth and Old Earth

LPI challenges the "how old" question itself.

- **Literal 24-hour days** from Earth's reference frame. Genesis means what it says.
- **Multi-threaded time architecture.** Cosmic processes genuinely occurred; stars really underwent nucleosynthesis, light really propagated. This is not apparent age (fake history) but compressed history (real processes, different temporal parameters).
- **Pre-Fall period of unspecified duration.** Scripture provides no chronology between Creation and Fall.

The question reframed: "How old is the universe?" assumes a single uniform timeline. But if God coordinated different reference frames during Creation Week, the question has no simple answer.

## II. The Core Framework

LPI operates on three foundational principles:

### LITERAL

Scripture means what it says. The "evening and morning" formula repeats six times, establishing literal 24-hour days. Jesus affirmed recent creation: "From the beginning of creation, 'God made them male and female'" (Mark 10:6).

### PROGRAMMATIC

Natural systems exhibit design characteristics. Physical laws demonstrate mathematical precision; genetic systems encode information; ecological networks maintain homeostasis.

The claim is structural, not substrate-dependent. LPI does not assert that the universe runs on digital bits. The claim is that rational ordering, rule-governed behavior, and information-bearing structures are genuine features of creation because creation proceeds from rational agency.

### INTERVENTION

God strategically coordinates His creation to accelerate natural processes, align complex systems, and achieve specific outcomes. Rather than suspending physics, God demonstrates mastery over temporal parameters, initial conditions, and systemic coordination.

## III. Biblical Precedent for Divine Temporal Manipulation

Scripture establishes clear precedent for God's mastery over time and process:

- **Joshua's Extended Day** (Joshua 10:13): Temporal parameters altered while maintaining system integrity
- **Hezekiah's Reversed Shadow** (Isaiah 38:8): Time itself manipulated as a sign
- **Jesus' Interventions:** Wine at Cana (instantaneous chemical transformation), multiplication of food, healing miracles (cellular regeneration accelerated), fig tree judgment (decay processes accelerated)

The pattern: God preserves natural processes while altering their temporal parameters.

## IV. Creation Week: The Multi-Threaded Time Proposal

Genesis 1:14-19 describes celestial bodies created on Day 4. This presents the starlight problem: observations suggest stellar processes requiring billions of years under uniform physical laws.

By analogy to modern computational systems that manage different processes at radically different temporal rates while maintaining causal coherence:

- **Earth Reference Frame:** Standard rotation, 24-hour day-night cycles
- **Cosmic Reference Frame:** Stellar nucleosynthesis, galactic dynamics executing on compressed timelines
- **Synchronization Point:** By Day 4's end, cosmic processes achieve intended configuration

**Honest Acknowledgment:** This is miraculous divine action on temporal parameters, not merely analogical. LPI does not attempt to place multi-threaded time inside physics; it places it inside providence.

The distinction between "apparent age" (fake history) and "compressed history" (processes that genuinely happened but at different temporal rates) is real and philosophically precise.

## V. The Pre-Fall Population Model

Adam and Eve were the singular progenitors of all humanity. However, Scripture provides no chronology between Creation and the Fall, allowing for substantial time during which genetically perfect offspring reproduced and spread across the pre-Flood supercontinent.

### Adam's Threefold Position

- **First among many:** Adam was the singular origin
- **Federal head:** Adam served as covenantal representative of all humanity
- **Assigned steward of creation:** The dominion mandate placed creation under Adam's authority

### Guilt vs. Consequences

Guilt is personal culpability for the act itself. Adam alone bears guilt. Consequences are the state of affairs resulting from the act. All under Adam's stewardship experience these.

The analogy: A wealthy man squanders his riches. The children are not guilty of the squandering, but they inherit the impoverished estate.

## VI. The Flood: Hydraulic Catastrophe

### The Hydrotectonic Collapse Model

Genesis 7:11: "All the fountains of the great deep burst forth."

LPI proposes the pre-Flood Earth possessed different crustal architecture: a water-saturated lithosphere with distributed liquid water throughout crustal aquifers.

**Observational Confirmation:** In 2014, seismological studies confirmed Earth's mantle transition zone contains 1-3 ocean masses of water structurally bound in high-pressure minerals (Pearson et al., 2014, Nature 507:221-224). Genesis predicted massive subsurface water millennia before modern geophysics discovered it.

### Thermal Management

Previous catastrophic plate models failed on thermodynamic grounds. Hydraulic collapse bypasses this:

- **Friction collapse:** When pore pressures approach lithostatic values, friction drops from 0.6-0.85 (dry rock) to ~0.01 (water-lubricated)
- **Energy dissipation in water:** Heat capacity of water is ~4× higher than silicate
- **Quantitative heat budget:** Moving continental blocks ~1000 km produces ~10²³ J, distributed over Earth's surface over one year: ~7 W/m². Compare to solar input of 340 W/m². Temperature increase: ~1 K.

## VII. Addressing Key Objections

### Radiometric Dating

Radiometric dating relies on assumptions about initial conditions, closed systems, and constant decay rates. The Lakatosian structure reveals that deep-time chronology has a hard core protected by auxiliary hypotheses. When results match expectations, the method works. When results contradict expectations, auxiliary explanations preserve the hard core.

### Fossil Sequence

The catastrophic alternative proposes hydrodynamic sorting combined with ecological zonation from the pre-Flood world. Honest assessment: fine-scale biostratigraphy presents significant challenges. This remains a research frontier.

### Parsimony

The objection assumes naturalism is the simple baseline. Both frameworks carry substantial complexity commitments:

**Naturalism's hidden commitments:**
- Fine-tuning requires infinite unobservable universes
- Abiogenesis requires unknown unguided pathway
- Information requires random mutations generating functional novelty
- Consciousness requires mind from matter

LPI posits one transcendent rational agent acting with purpose. Which framework is actually simpler?

## VIII. Conclusion

Literal Programmatic Intervention stands as a framework that integrates biblical fidelity, scientific engagement, and epistemological honesty.

- **Biblical fidelity:** Taking Genesis literally, preserving theological integrity by placing death after sin
- **Scientific engagement:** Providing physical mechanisms for catastrophic events
- **Epistemological honesty:** Grounding the framework in the Logos-Intelligibility argument

The fundamental insight: biblical theism is not one interpretation struggling for credibility alongside others. It is the framework that explains why interpretation, evidence, and reasoning are possible at all.
