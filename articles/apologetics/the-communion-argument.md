# The Communion Argument: An Evidence-Based Case for Trinitarian Theism

**James (JD) Longmire**

## Abstract

This paper introduces and defends a novel argument for theism – the Communion Argument – which proceeds from the empirically well-established fact that human beings are constitutively relational. Drawing on convergent evidence from developmental psychology, social neuroscience, epidemiology, anthropology, and phenomenology, I argue that the human orientation toward personal communion requires metaphysical explanation.

I evaluate two candidate explanations: (A) naturalism, which treats relational constitution as an ungrounded evolutionary accident, and (B) theism, which grounds it in an ultimate reality that is itself personal and relational. I argue that theism provides a superior explanation.

Further, I demonstrate that the specifically Trinitarian conception of God is evidentially established through historical testimony concerning Jesus of Nazareth, the evidence for his resurrection, and the early church's experience of the Spirit.

---

## 1. Introduction

The classical arguments for God's existence proceed from various starting points: contingency, design, morality, or consciousness. This paper introduces a distinct argument – the Communion Argument – which begins from a different datum: the constitutively relational nature of human persons.

Human beings are not isolated individuals who contingently enter into relationships; rather, we are made for communion. Our deepest fulfilments and our deepest sufferings are personal and relational. This is not merely philosophical speculation but an empirically robust finding confirmed across multiple disciplines.

---

## 2. The Evidential Base: Human Beings as Constitutively Relational

### 2.1 Developmental Psychology: Attachment Theory

Bowlby's attachment theory demonstrates that infants are biologically predisposed to seek proximity to caregivers, and that the quality of early attachment relationships shapes psychological development across the lifespan. The Minnesota Longitudinal Study, spanning over 35 years, demonstrates that early attachment security predicts relationship quality, emotional regulation, and psychological health throughout adulthood.

### 2.2 Social Neuroscience: The Relational Brain

Functional neuroimaging studies demonstrate that social rejection activates the same neural regions as physical pain. Excluded participants showed increased activity in regions consistently associated with the affective component of physical pain. The neural overlap suggests that social connection is literally essential to human wellbeing.

### 2.3 Epidemiology: Social Connection and Mortality

Holt-Lunstad et al. (2010), in a meta-analysis of 148 prospective studies involving 308,849 participants, found that individuals with stronger social relationships had a 50% greater likelihood of survival. The mortality risk associated with social disconnection is comparable to smoking up to 15 cigarettes per day and exceeds the risk associated with obesity and physical inactivity.

### 2.4 Solitary Confinement: The Extreme Case

Grassian (1983) identified a specific syndrome in solitary confinement characterized by perceptual distortions, panic attacks, difficulty thinking, memory problems, and paranoia. The United Nations has called for a ban on solitary confinement exceeding 15 days, characterizing prolonged isolation as cruel, inhuman, and degrading treatment.

## 3. The Argument's Formal Structure

**Premise 1 (Empirical):** Human beings are constitutively relational. We are made for personal communion in a way that shapes our development, cognition, health, and flourishing.

**Premise 2 (Disjunctive):** This relational constitution is either:
- (A) Ungrounded: an accident of evolutionary processes, useful for survival but pointing to nothing ultimate; or
- (B) Grounded: a reflection of and participation in ultimate reality, which is itself personal and relational.

**Premise 3 (Evaluative):** Option (B) better explains the evidence because:
- It explains why communion fulfils beyond survival value
- It explains why isolation devastates beyond reproductive cost
- It explains why persons are experienced as irreducibly different from objects
- It explains the universality of relational structures across human cultures

**Premise 4 (Theological):** If ultimate reality is personal and relational, then God is not solitary but inherently communal. This is precisely what Trinitarian theism claims.

**Conclusion:** The evidence of human relational constitution supports Trinitarian theism over naturalism.

## 4. The Evidential Basis for the Trinitarian Specification

The Trinity is not arbitrary theological speculation. It is the only formulation that holds together the evidential constraints:

### Evidence Streams:
1. **Jewish Monotheism:** The disciples were strict monotheists who would not worship a human unless compelled by evidence
2. **Jesus's Self-Understanding:** Claims to divine prerogatives (forgiving sins, pre-existence, unity with the Father)
3. **The Resurrection:** Early testimony, multiple attestation, empty tomb, post-resurrection appearances, transformation of disciples
4. **The Spirit's Distinct Personhood:** Described with personal attributes (can be lied to, grieved, has a mind)
5. **Triadic Pattern in Early Practice:** Baptismal formula, benedictions, doxologies

### Competing Explanations Fail:

| Hypothesis | Problem |
|------------|---------|
| Modalism | Contradicts the distinctions: Jesus prays to the Father |
| Tritheism | Contradicts Jewish monotheism |
| Subordinationism | Contradicts evidence of full divinity |
| Adoptionism | Contradicts pre-existence claims |
| **Trinity** | Accounts for all the data without contradiction |

## 5. The Therapeutic Confirmation

Despite unprecedented advances in psychology and psychiatry, rates of loneliness, anxiety, depression, and suicide continue to rise. We have more therapeutic resources than ever before, yet outcomes have not improved proportionally.

This is exactly what we would expect if our wounds are personal rather than functional, requiring reconciliation rather than recalibration.

### What Theism Offers That Naturalism Cannot:

- **To Be Known, Not Just Assessed:** A God who knows us by name
- **To Be Healed, Not Just Managed:** Actual healing, not symptom management
- **To Be Forgiven, Not Just Reframed:** Actual forgiveness from the One against whom all sin is committed
- **To Belong, Not Just Connect:** Incorporation into a body, not networking
- **To Matter, Not Just Be Validated:** Significance grounded in the image of God

## 6. Conclusion

The evidence is clear: human beings are made for communion. Either our relational nature is an ungrounded accident, or it reflects the structure of reality itself. The second explanation is superior.

If ultimate reality is personal and relational, then God is communion – eternally relational in the divine nature. This is precisely what Trinitarian Christianity claims. And this claim is evidentially grounded in the testimony of those who encountered Jesus, witnessed his resurrection, and experienced the Spirit as a distinct divine person.

We are made for communion because we are made by Communion: the eternal self-giving love of Father, Son, and Spirit, from whose overflow we are created and into whose life we are invited.
