# Comparing Theism to Atheistic Naturalism

*Note: The original Substack post for this article consisted primarily of a visual comparison table/infographic. The content below summarizes the key comparative points.*

## The Fundamental Question

When comparing theism to atheistic naturalism, we're comparing two comprehensive frameworks for understanding reality. Each makes claims about:

- The origin of the universe
- The nature of consciousness
- The basis for morality
- The reliability of reason
- The meaning of human existence

## Key Comparison Points

### Origin and Existence

**Theism:** The universe exists because a necessary, eternal, rational Mind brought it into being with purpose and design.

**Naturalism:** The universe exists as a brute fact, requiring no external explanation. Everything that exists is ultimately physical.

### Consciousness

**Theism:** Mind is fundamental to reality. Human consciousness reflects the divine Mind that created it.

**Naturalism:** Consciousness is an emergent property of sufficiently complex physical processes. No mechanism explains how subjective experience arises from objective matter.

### Morality

**Theism:** Objective moral truths exist, grounded in God's nature and character. Moral obligations are real and binding.

**Naturalism:** Morality is either subjective (personal preference), conventional (social agreement), or an evolutionary adaptation with no objective grounding.

### Reason

**Theism:** Reason is reliable because we are made in the image of a rational God to understand a rationally ordered creation.

**Naturalism:** Reason is a product of blind evolutionary processes selected for survival, not truth. This raises questions about whether we can trust our reasoning faculties.

### Meaning

**Theism:** Human life has objective purpose and significance because we are created beings with a designated role in God's plan.

**Naturalism:** Meaning is subjective or constructed. The universe has no inherent purpose; any meaning must be self-generated.

## The Explanatory Comparison

Theism provides a unified explanation for:
- Why the universe exists
- Why it is rationally ordered
- Why consciousness exists
- Why moral intuitions are universal
- Why meaning-seeking is intrinsic to human nature

Naturalism must treat each of these as separate brute facts or unsolved problems, offering no unifying explanation for why reality exhibits these features.

## The Bottom Line

The comparison isn't between faith and reason, but between two faith commitments:
- Faith that mind preceded matter
- Faith that matter preceded mind

Only one of these provides a coherent foundation for the very reasoning we use to evaluate worldviews.
