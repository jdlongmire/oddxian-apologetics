# Examining the Evidence for God

## The Evidence for God

"There's No Evidence for God"? Really? Let's Look.

Evidence isn't just test tubes and telescopes. It's logic, mathematics, information, consciousness. Everything naturalism can't explain but theism predicts.

---

**Here's the evidence:**

**1. The universe began**  
Every physicist agrees: spacetime had a birthday. Whatever begins requires a cause. The cause of spacetime can't be spatial or temporal. It must be spaceless, timeless, immaterial, powerful. Exactly what theists call God.

**2. Fine-tuning isn't fine. It's absurd**  
Change gravity by 1 part in 10^60, no stars. Alter the cosmological constant by 1 part in 10^120, no universe. This isn't "fine" tuning. It's telepathic-archer-hitting-an-atom-across-the-universe tuning. Design or miracle odds. Pick one.

**3. Mathematics predicts reality**  
Dirac's equation predicted antimatter. Einstein's math predicted black holes. Maxwell's equations predicted radio waves. The universe doesn't just follow math. It *is* math. Mind, not matter, speaks mathematics.

**4. Information requires intelligence**  
Every cell contains 3 billion base pairs encoding functional information. Not patterns. *Instructions*. We've never seen instructions write themselves. Ever. Books require authors. Code requires coders. DNA requires intelligence.

**5. Consciousness can't be computed**  
No amount of circuits produces first-person experience. Computation shuffles symbols; consciousness experiences meaning. You can't get "what it's like" from "what it does." Matter doesn't wake up.

**6. Moral facts exist**  
Torturing babies for fun is wrong. Not just unpopular, *wrong*. The Holocaust wasn't just "not our preference." If morality is real, it needs grounding beyond human opinion. Physical facts can't generate moral facts. Only moral beings can.

**7. Reason itself requires God**  
If our brains are just chemical accidents, why trust them? Evolution selects for survival, not truth. A brain evolved to avoid predators has no business doing quantum physics. Unless mind designed mind.

**8. The universe is comprehensible**  
Why should ape brains understand cosmic origins? Why should math work at all? Why are physical laws simple enough to write on a napkin? Chaos doesn't produce comprehensibility. Intelligence does.

**9. Beauty, love, and meaning persist**  
If we're just atoms, love = oxytocin, beauty = neural firing patterns, meaning = delusion. Yet no one lives that way. Everyone acts like these things are real. Because they are.

**10. Changed lives across millennia**  
Billions report direct experience of God. Transformed addicts, healed marriages, radical forgiveness, unexplainable peace. Mass delusion? Or mass evidence you're ignoring?

---

**The Combined Case:**

We have:

- A universe that shouldn't exist
- Fine-tuning that defies probability
- Mathematical elegance underlying chaos
- Information without material source
- Consciousness without material cause
- Moral facts without material grounding
- Reason that trusts itself without justification
- Comprehensibility without explanation
- Universal human experiences that materialism denies
- Billions of testimonies across cultures and time

**That's not proof. But it's evidence.**

Mountains of it.

---

## The Evidence for the Christian God

**"But Which God?"**

Fair question. Let's get specific.

**The Evidence for the Christian God:**

**1. The Jewish anomaly**  
An insignificant tribe predicted they'd bless all nations, survive every empire, and return to their homeland after exile. Babylon fell. Rome fell. The Jews remained. Israel exists. Statistical impossibility or prophetic fulfillment?

**2. Messianic fingerprints**  
Over 300 prophecies. Born in Bethlehem (Micah 5:2). Killed by crucifixion, predicted before crucifixion existed (Psalm 22). Arrives 483 years after rebuilding Jerusalem (Daniel 9). The odds? 1 in 10^157. That's more than atoms in the universe.

**3. The resurrection problem**  
The disciples died claiming they saw Jesus alive. Not "spiritual experience." Physical meetings. Liars don't die for lies they invented. Hallucinations don't happen to 500 people at once. The tomb was empty. Rome couldn't produce a body. Jerusalem couldn't explain it.

**4. The manuscript mountain**  
5,800+ Greek manuscripts. 10,000+ Latin. 500+ Aramaic. Compare: Homer's Iliad has 1,900. Caesar's Gallic Wars? 10. We have better evidence for Jesus' words than for any ancient figure.

**5. The transformation pattern**  
Saul the persecutor becomes Paul the apostle. Constantine the pagan becomes defender of faith. Augustine the hedonist becomes theologian. Millions report the same pattern: encounter, transformation, purpose. Mass psychology? Or mass evidence?

**6. The hostile witnesses**  
Tacitus, Pliny, Josephus, the Talmud. Enemies of Christianity confirm: Jesus existed, was crucified under Pilate, followers claimed resurrection, movement exploded. Even critics couldn't deny the basics.

**7. The hunger fit**  
Every culture seeks God. Every heart craves purpose. Every conscience knows guilt. Christianity doesn't just answer questions. It explains why we ask them. The lock proves the key exists.

**8. The person of Christ**  
Peasant carpenter splits history. Illiterate fishermen write timeless literature. Movement without weapons conquers empire. Jesus claimed to be God and rose to prove it. Lunatic, liar, or Lord. Those are your options.

**9. Present power**  
Addictions broken. Marriages restored. Depression lifted. Hatred transformed to love. Not self-help: surrender. Not psychology: power. Millions. Daily. Globally. Documented.

**10. The explanatory scope**  
Christianity explains:

- Why the universe exists (Creation)
- Why it's broken (Fall)
- Why we long for better (Eden echo)
- Why God allows suffering (Love requires freedom)
- Why Jesus matters (God entering the rescue)
- Why it will end well (Restoration coming)

**No other worldview explains more with less.**

## The Final Analysis

**The Test:**

Christianity makes falsifiable claims:

- If Christ didn't rise, it's false
- If the tomb wasn't empty, it's false
- If the prophecies failed, it's false
- If lives aren't changed, it's false

2,000 years later? Still standing. Still transforming. Still explaining.

**The Bottom Line:**

Naturalism believes in:

- Something from nothing
- Life from non-life
- Mind from matter
- Morality from molecules
- Order from chaos
- Everything from nothing

Christianity believes in:

- Mind before matter
- Life from Life
- Design from Designer
- Purpose from Person
- Order from Orderer
- Everything from Everything

**One requires blind faith in nothing creating everything.**  
**One requires evidence-based faith in Everything creating everything.**

The question isn't "Where's the evidence?"

The question is "Why are you ignoring it?"

Science explores *how*.  
The evidence points to *Who*.  
History reveals *which Who*.

What's your excuse for not looking?

We don't reject science. We reject the philosophy wearing its lab coat.

Theism makes one claim: Mind precedes matter.

Christianity makes one addition: That Mind entered matter.

The evidence agrees.
