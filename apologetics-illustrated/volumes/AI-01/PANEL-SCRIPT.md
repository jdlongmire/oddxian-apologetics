## The objection

- **As asked:** "The Bible is just an ancient man-made book, copied, translated, and edited for centuries. Why trust it?"
- **Source:** common charge, no single source; it is the standing objection the corpus volume BWM-IS-03 was written against.
- **Strongest form:** Even if the original authors claimed divine authority, what we hold is the product of fallible copying, translation, and church decision across two thousand years, so the chain between God's speech and our page is too weak to bear trust.
- **One-line answer:** The chain is the point: God breathed out the words through real human authors, and the same providence that gave them has stewarded them in the open, through witnesses we can examine, a canon the church recognized rather than invented, and a word that endures.

## Governing claim and corpus basis

Built from JD's corpus, not generic apologetics material. Principal sources: BWM-IS-03 panel script and companion narrative (biblical-worldmodel/illustrated-systematics/volumes/BWM-IS-03/); the foundational treatise Prolegomena (arguments-frameworks/foundational-treatise/parts/part-00-prolegomena.md), sections 3, 4.2, and 4.5 (hierarchy of authority, the WCF 1.6 rule of good and necessary consequence, the strongest-form rule). IS-03 teaches divine authorship as doctrine; this volume defends the Bible's trustworthiness against the objection above. Each cross-references the other.

**Corpus discipline carried into this script (from the IS-03 narrative):** inspiration is explicit biblical teaching; providential preservation is a theological inference and is labeled so; canon recognition is a historical and theological synthesis whose confessional formulation IS-03's own register rates UNCERTAIN pending historical sourcing, so panel 7 is worded at that strength; textual transmission is historical evidence, handled by ordinary textual-critical methods, and no panel claims that every copy, translation, or canon decision was infallible.

## Corpus gaps flagged for JD (also in the report and the narrative)

1. **Empirical manuscript data is thin in the corpus.** Counts of manuscripts, earliest fragments, Dead Sea Scrolls evidence: none of it is in IS-03 or the treatise, and the IS-03 narrative lists exactly that historical research as still required before candidate status. This plate therefore makes no numeric manuscript claims. If JD wants them on the plate, that research has to be done and sourced first.
2. **Canon formulation confidence.** IS-03's own claim register rates "the church recognizes rather than creates divine authority" UNCERTAIN pending historical review. Panel 7 keeps the register's wording and strength.
3. **Preservation mechanism.** Corpus position: preservation is inference from sovereignty plus the enduring word, not a demonstrated mechanism. Panels 5 and 6 keep the promise and the inference visibly separate.
