# Companion Narrative Template: Apologetics Illustrated

One file per volume, at `volumes/AI-##/NARRATIVE.md`. The narrative substantiates the plate. A volume cannot reach candidate status without it.

## Header

- **Volume:** AI-##, title
- **Byline:** James (JD) Longmire / oddXian Apologetics Ministry
- **Plate:** link to the volume's plate file
- **Status:** draft / candidate / JD-approved, with dates

## 1. The objection

The charge stated in its strongest form, quoted or cited from its real source where one exists (author, work, locator). If the objection is a common charge, say so and give its usual form.

## 2. The answer in brief

The defense in a short paragraph: what is conceded, what is denied, and where the argument turns.

## 3. The argument

The full defense, section by section, in the order the panels present it. Scripture handled grammatically and historically; corpus arguments cited by path and section; external sources by exact locator. Qualifications and corpus verdicts on the source arguments are recorded here, not hidden.

## 6. Anticipated replies
