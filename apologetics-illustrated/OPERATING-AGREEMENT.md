# Operating Agreement: Apologetics Illustrated

## Authority

JD is doctrinal editor-in-chief, asset acceptance authority, and publication approver. He holds doctrinal and publication approval for every volume in this program; no merge to main and no publication happens without his word. Scripture is normative. Arguments, inferences, and working hypotheses must be visibly distinguished from direct teaching, in the plate and in its companion narrative.

## Thinx-Muse: process and repository operations

Under JD's direction, thinx-muse runs the process for this program:

1. Plan volumes from the series index: argument map first, then the panel script, then the plate.
2. Draft panel scripts and companion narratives, including the objection stated in its strongest form, claim classifications, ESV reference and quotation checks, and panel-to-source maps.
3. Produce plates, run visual QA under the adopted QA process, and record every audit in QA-LOG.md.
4. Maintain the series index and asset manifest; compute SHA-256 hashes and record actual file sizes, dimensions, and provenance for every ingested asset.
5. Manage branches and PRs within existing repository governance; report evidence of each operation. Do not infer JD approval.
6. Record final disposition, supersession chain, and publication target for every volume.

Other agents contribute only as JD directs for a specific task.

## Category discipline

This program is defending the faith. Before a volume is planned, its framing objection or rival account is named in the series index. If a planned plate turns out to be doctrinal exposition with no objection in view, it is transferred to Illustrated Systematics, not published here. Cross-references between the two programs are welcome; duplicate filing is not.

## Disposition rules

- **draft:** produced, unverified
- **candidate:** checked and ready for JD review
- **approved:** explicit JD approval recorded
- **published:** publication destination and immutable commit recorded
- **superseded/rejected:** reason, reviewer, and replacement recorded

No force pushes, unauthorized merges, silent overwrites, or changes to doctrinal canon without review. Repairs to lettering or artwork are always disclosed in the QA log; originals are never altered silently.

## Narrative and source research

Every volume requires a companion narrative before candidate status, following the sourcing policy: the objection quoted or cited from a real source where one exists, the response argued from Scripture and the corpus, claims classified, and every panel mapped to its source. No invented links, citations, or quotations.
