# Panel Script Template: Apologetics Illustrated

One file per volume, at `volumes/AI-##/PANEL-SCRIPT.md`. The script is the source of truth for the plate; the plate is generated or assembled from it, and QA verifies the plate against it element by element.

## Header block

- **Volume ID:** AI-##
- **Title:**
- **Framing objection (as asked):** the charge or question in the words a critic would use
- **Objection source:** named critic and locator, or "common charge, no single source"
- **Objection, strongest form:** two or three sentences, steelmanned
- **One-line answer:** the defense in a single sentence
- **Seed sources:** corpus paths this volume is built from
- **Shirt color (style guide rotation):**
- **Status:** draft / candidate / JD-approved

## Plate anatomy

Header: main title, subtitle, framing box carrying the objection and the one-line answer, book stack.
Panels 1 to 9: each panel advances the defense by one step. Where a panel voices the objection, mark it OBJECTION; where it answers, mark it RESPONSE.
Closing panel: The Bottom Line: checklist of the defense's load-bearing points, and a closing quotation with its reference.
Footer: oddXian.com and the volume ID, with the host in friendly instruction mode (Teaching pose). The header portrait is the host in contemplation mode (Thinking/Reflecting pose).

Production conventions (JD rulings, 2026-11-01):

- Panel numbers appear only in the gold badge; panel title text never carries a number. The closing panel, which has no badge, carries its number in its title.
- The objection is never voiced by the host; it is carried by the panel's quotation card and caption over a content scene.
- Host placement: the host appears only in the header (contemplation mode) and the footer (friendly instruction mode). Body panels carry content scenes with no host image; write each panel's Scene line as content only.

## Per-panel block (repeat for panels 1 to 9, then the closing panel)

- **Panel N title:**
- **Kind:** OBJECTION / RESPONSE / TURN (the panel where the argument turns)
- **Scene:** what the illustration shows (symbolic rather than anthropomorphic for divine Persons)
- **Bubble text:** exact quotation, or a summary explicitly labeled as a summary
- **Bubble reference:** book chapter:verse (ESV), or the cited work and locator
- **Caption:** the takeaway, every line as it will appear on the plate
- **Claim:** the panel's claim, classified per the sourcing policy, with its register ID (##-N)
- **Source locator:** corpus path and section, or external primary source

## Pre-generation check

- Objection stated as its best defenders state it, with a source.
- Every quotation verified against the ESV or the cited work.
- Every claim classified; nothing classified higher than its evidence.
- No panel teaches doctrine for its own sake; every panel serves the answer. (Doctrine the answer depends on is cited to Illustrated Systematics or the treatise, not re-taught here.)
