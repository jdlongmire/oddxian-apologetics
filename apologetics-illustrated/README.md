## The category

This repository carries two distinct illustrated programs, and they are not the same kind of work:

- **Illustrated Systematics** (`biblical-worldmodel/illustrated-systematics/`) is biblical theology and doctrine: what the faith teaches, set out in order.
- **Apologetics Illustrated** (this section) is **defending the faith**: arguments answered, objections met.

A plate belongs to this program only if its job is defense: it takes up a charge, an objection, or a rival account of things, and answers it. Doctrinal exposition, however useful to a defense, belongs to Illustrated Systematics. The two programs share a visual language, a QA standard, and a sourcing discipline; they do not share a category, and material is not filed under both.

## What a volume is

One volume is one defense on one plate: a ten-panel illustrated sheet built around a framing question (the objection as it is actually asked), with a companion narrative that carries the full argument, the objection stated in its strongest form, the claim register, and the panel-to-source map. Plates summarize; narratives substantiate.
