## Header

Title: THE BIBLE: DIVINE AUTHORSHIP AND PROVIDENTIAL STEWARDSHIP
Subtitle: God's Written Revelation, Authored by the Spirit, Given Through Human Instruments, and Preserved by His Providence for His People.
Header checklist: The Bible is our material source of divine truth - a trustworthy, authoritative, and preserved revelation from God to His people.
Book spines: AUTHORSHIP / INSPIRATION / CANON / PRESERVATION / AUTHORITY

## Panel 1: GOD HAS SPOKEN

Quote: "In many and various ways God spoke of old to our fathers by the prophets, but in these last days He has spoken to us by His Son..." - Hebrews 1:1-2
Caption: God is not silent. He has revealed Himself, His purpose, and His will, culminating in Jesus Christ.

## Panel 2: THE SPIRIT, DIVINE AUTHOR

Quote: "All Scripture is God-breathed and profitable for teaching... that the man of God may be complete..." - 2 Timothy 3:16-17
Caption: The Holy Spirit is the divine Author of Scripture, sovereignly breathing out God's truth without error.

## Panel 3: INSPIRED THROUGH HUMAN AUTHORS

Quote: "Men spoke from God as they were carried along by the Holy Spirit." - 2 Peter 1:20-21
Caption: God's Word came through real people - prophets, apostles, and others - fully human, truly authors, and supernaturally inspired.

## Panel 4: THE SPIRIT'S PROVIDENTIAL STEWARDSHIP

Quote: "But the Helper, the Holy Spirit... will teach you all things and bring to your remembrance all that I have said to you." - John 14:26
Caption: The Spirit not only inspired the text but also stewards God's revelation - guiding His people, preserving the truth, and opening understanding.

## Panel 5: THE CANON: RECOGNIZED THROUGH FALLEN HUMANITY

Quote: "...the things concerning Him... written in the Law of Moses and the Prophets and the Psalms..." - Luke 24:44
Caption: The canon is the collection of God-breathed writings, recognized by His people through historical examination, use, and discernment under the Spirit's guidance, even through fallible men.

## Panel 6: TRANSMISSION AND PRESERVATION

Quote: "The grass withers, the flower fades, but the word of our God will stand forever." - Isaiah 40:8
Caption: Through centuries of copying, distribution, and use, God has providentially preserved His Word, so we have a reliable and substantially complete text today.

## Panel 7: SCRIPTURE INTERPRETS SCRIPTURE

Quote: "And beginning with Moses and all the Prophets, He interpreted to them in all the Scriptures the things concerning Himself." - Luke 24:27
Caption: The Bible is a unified and coherent whole. Clear passages inform more difficult ones, with Christ at the center of its message.

## Panel 8: ILLUMINATION, LOGIC, AND SOUND HERMENEUTICS

Checklist: Illumination (by the Spirit) / Analogy of Faith / Good and Necessary Consequence / Parsimony / Progressive Revelation
Caption: We understand Scripture by the Spirit's illumination, using sound interpretive principles that respect context, coherence, and the unfolding of God's revelation.

## Panel 9: SCRIPTURE: OUR AUTHORITATIVE MATERIAL SOURCE

Quote: "They received the word with all eagerness, examining the Scriptures daily to see if these things were so." - Acts 17:11
Caption: Scripture is our final and sufficient authority for faith and life. It defines truth, reveals the gospel, shapes doctrine and ethics, and anchors our hope in Christ.

## Panel 10: THE BIG PICTURE

Checklist:
- Author: The Holy Spirit, the divine Author of Scripture.
- Instrument: Real human authors, inspired and accountable.
- Steward: The Spirit guides, preserves, and illumines God's Word.
- Canon: Recognized by God's people through historical discernment.
- Preservation: Providentially maintained through fallible humanity.
- Interpretation: By the Spirit, through sound and faithful hermeneutics.
- Authority: Our material source of divine truth for faith and life.
