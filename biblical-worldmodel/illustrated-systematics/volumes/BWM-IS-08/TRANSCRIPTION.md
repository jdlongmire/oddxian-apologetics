## Header

Title: THE FALL: FROM COMMUNION TO REBELLION (REBELLION in gold). Marker: VOL. 08.
Subtitle: The Command Questioned, the Fruit Taken, the Presence Fled.
Framing box: Eden's communion did not collapse from outside. A question was entertained, God's word was recast, desire re-judged the tree, and the hand took what the will had already taken. What follows is rebellion, and, inside the sentence itself, the first promise.
Host portrait: red shirt.

## Panels

Panel 1, THE SERPENT'S QUESTION. Book stack: SCRIPTURE / FALL / SIN / REDEMPTION. Bubble: "Did God actually say, 'You shall not eat of any tree in the garden'?" Caption: The attack opens on God's word, not on the fruit. The command is recast as deprivation: one withheld tree becomes, in the question, every tree. Reference: Genesis 3:1.

Panel 2, THE COMMAND RECOUNTED, AND ADDED TO. Bubble: "We may eat of the fruit of the trees in the garden." Caption: Eve answers truly, and adds a fence God did not state: neither shall you touch it. The command is already being handled at a distance from the Giver's own words. Reference: Genesis 3:2-3.

Panel 3, DEATH DENIED, GODHOOD OFFERED. Bubble: "You will not surely die." Caption: The sanction is denied outright, and the withheld knowledge is offered as elevation: you will be like God. Distrust of God's goodness is the doorway to disobedience. Reference: Genesis 3:4-5.

Panel 4, SEEN, DESIRED, JUDGED GOOD. Bubble: "It was a delight to the eyes, and... to be desired to make one wise." Caption: Before the hand moves, the verdict changes. Good for food, a delight, desirable for wisdom: creaturely judgment now sits above God's command. The evil will precedes the evil deed. Reference: Genesis 3:6.

Panel 5, SHE ATE, AND HE ATE. Bubble: "She took of its fruit and ate, and she also gave some to her husband." Caption: The act itself is told in a breath. She ate, she gave, he ate: the head of the covenant eats with his wife, and the breach is complete in both. Reference: Genesis 3:6.

Panel 6, EYES OPENED, SHAME ENTERED. Bubble: "Then the eyes of both were opened, and they knew that they were naked." Caption: The promised opening of eyes comes, and it is shame. The knowledge seized brings exposure, not godhood, and the first human craft is a covering. Reference: Genesis 3:7.

Panel 7, HIDING FROM THE PRESENCE. Bubble: "I was afraid, because I was naked, and I hid myself." Caption: Communion becomes flight. The voice that once meant fellowship is now feared, and Adam's own confession names the change: fear, nakedness, hiding. Reference: Genesis 3:8-10.

Panel 8, THE QUESTION AND THE BLAME. Bubble: "The woman whom you gave to be with me, she gave me fruit." Caption: God questions, and confession bends into blame: the woman, and behind her the Giver, are indicted. Eve answers in kind: the serpent deceived me. Sin now speaks in excuses. Reference: Genesis 3:11-13.

Panel 9, JUDGMENT, AND A PROMISE INSIDE IT. Bubble: "I will put enmity between you and the woman, and between your offspring and her offspring." Reference line inside the bubble, as generated: Reference: Genesis 3:14-19 (repaired, see production note). Caption: The sentence falls in order: serpent, woman, man. Inside the serpent's judgment stands the first promise: the woman's seed will bruise the serpent's head. Grace is announced before exile is spoken.

Panel 10, THE BIG PICTURE. Host in red shirt. Checklist: Questioned: God's word recast as deprivation. / Distrusted: Death denied, godhood believed. / Taken: The will consented before the hand. / Ashamed: Eyes opened to nakedness, not glory. / Hidden: Communion fled, blame spoken. / Promised: The seed will bruise the serpent. Closing verse: "He shall bruise your head, and you shall bruise his heel." Reference: Genesis 3:15.

## Production note

The raw generation rendered Adam in panels 7 and 8 with the host's features (bald, glasses, white goatee; panel 8 also in modern dress), contrary to the series standard that the host appears in the header and closing panel only. Both scenes were regenerated as standalone scenes with a young, dark-haired, clean-shaven Adam and composited into the panels' scene rectangles only, with the panels' original speech bubbles transplanted over the new scenes on masks segmented from the bubbles themselves; no lettering was re-rendered in either panel. The raw generation also placed a reference line inside the panel 9 bubble reading "Genesis 3:4-19"; the defective segment only ("3:4-19", ink box x 1212-1253, y 1299-1316) was covered with the bubble's own white and re-lettered "3:14-19" matched in height to the measured ink box. The re-lettered segment sits in a slightly more condensed typeface than the surrounding generated lettering. A pixel diff against the raw generation shows change confined to the two scene rectangles and the panel 9 segment (26 pixels above the reporting threshold outside them, attributable to JPEG re-encoding noise). The ingested plate is therefore a repaired derivative of the raw generation, recorded here and in QA-LOG.md rather than passed off as a clean generation.
