## Governing claim

The communion of Eden was broken from inside the human will before it was broken in the outward act. Genesis 3 tells the breach in order: the serpent questions God's word (3:1), Eve recounts the command with an addition (3:2-3), the serpent denies death and offers godhood (3:4-5), the tree is re-judged by desire and the fruit is taken and shared (3:6), eyes are opened to shame (3:7), the man and woman hide from God's presence and shift blame under His questioning (3:8-13), and God speaks judgment on serpent, woman, and man, with the promise of the seed inside the serpent's judgment (3:14-19), then clothes the couple and guards the way to the tree of life (3:20-24). Doctrinal anchor: foundational treatise Part IV (Fall and Sin), secs. 1, 2, 9, and 10, with axioms IV.1 to IV.5 and III.5, at jdlongmire/oddxian-apologetics commit 4d0934f966a2982a99073220cabf56f9e05dfed5. Confessional anchor: Westminster Confession of Faith 6.1 and 6.2. Thesis T-IV.3 of the treatise governs the volume's reading: the Fall was man's own act and fault, and its root was pride, the evil will preceding the evil deed. The fuller root-of-sin thesis (the self closed in on itself) is BWM-IS-09's subject and is carried here only at synthesis grade, never as a motive asserted inside every act, per the discipline of treatise Part IV sec. 1. Genesis 3:15 carries JD's covenant sequence, ruled 2026-11-13: the covenant of grace is set at the Fall (the lapsarian covenant) and sealed by death (Hebrews 9:15); that framing is labeled synthesis in the narrative.
