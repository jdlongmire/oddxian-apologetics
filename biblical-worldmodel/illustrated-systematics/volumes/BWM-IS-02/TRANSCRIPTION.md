## Header

Title: THE ETERNAL DECREE AND COVENANT
Subtitle: God's Sovereign Purpose to Create, Redeem, and Consummate Loving Communion.
Header text: Before time, God purposed, decreed, and covenanted to create a people for Himself and to bring them to everlasting loving communion in Christ.
Book spines: SCRIPTURE / COVENANT / SOVEREIGNTY / REDEMPTION

## Panel 1: THE ETERNAL DECREE

Quote: "He works all things according to the counsel of His will..." - Ephesians 1:11
Caption: The eternal decree is God's all-encompassing, wise, and purposeful plan to glorify Himself by bringing many into everlasting communion with Him.

## Panel 2: THE ETERNAL COVENANT

Quote (no citation on the plate): "A covenant of love within the Godhead that grounds God's work in ceeation [sic] and redemption."
Caption: The eternal covenant (covenant of redemption) is the loving agreement within the Trinity to redeem a people, with the Son accomplishing redemption and the Spirit applying it.

## Panel 3: THE PURPOSE: LOVING COMMUNION

Quote: "...that we should be holy and blameless before Him in love..." - Ephesians 1:4
Caption: The decree and covenant share one purpose: to bring a redeemed people into everlasting, joyful, and freely embraced communion with God.

## Panel 4: THE COVENANT FRAMEWORK

Scroll labels: Covenant of Creation / Covenant of Obedience / Covenant of Grace
Caption: God's redemptive plan unfolds in a covenantal framework: creation (purpose and relationship), obedience (righteous life and blessing), and grace (redemption and restoration through Christ).

## Panel 5: SOVEREIGNTY AND REAL HUMAN AGENCY

Quote: "...work out your own salvation with fear and trembling..." - Philippians 2:12
Caption: God's decree is sovereign and certain, yet human beings are genuine moral agents who make real choices. God ordains the end without negating our responsible agency.

## Panel 6: CHRIST: THE COVENANT MEDIATOR

Quote: "For there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus." - 1 Timothy 2:5
Caption: The Son fulfills the covenant by His incarnation, perfect obedience, atoning death, and victorious resurrection, securing the covenant's promises for His people.

## Panel 7: THE SPIRIT: COVENANT APPLICATION

Quote: "...the Spirit of the promise, who is the guarantee of our inheritance..." - Ephesians 1:13-14
Caption: The Holy Spirit applies the benefits of the covenant - regeneration, faith, adoption, sanctification, and perseverance - bringing us into a living relationship with God.

## Panel 8: THE PEOPLE OF THE COVENANT

Quote: "You are a chosen people, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, God's own people..." - 1 Peter 2:9
Caption: The covenant gathers a people for God from every nation, creating a new family defined by faith in Christ, love for God, and love for one another.

## Panel 9: THE CONSUMMATION

Quote: "Behold, the dwelling place of God is with man... and they will be His people, and God Himself will be with them." - Revelation 21:3
Caption: The decree and covenant culminate in the new creation - sin removed, righteousness confirmed, and loving communion with God perfected forever.

## Panel 10: THE BIG PICTURE

Checklist:
- Source: The triune God, from eternity.
- Plan: The eternal decree to glorify God in loving communion.
- Covenant: The intra-trinitarian agreement of redemption and its historical outworking.
- Mediator: The Son who fulfills the covenant.
- Application: The Spirit who applies it to God's people.
- People: A redeemed, called, and transformed community.
- End: Everlasting, perfected loving communion with God.

Quote: "He chose us in Christ before the foundation of the world... according to the purpose of His will..." - Ephesians 1:4-5
