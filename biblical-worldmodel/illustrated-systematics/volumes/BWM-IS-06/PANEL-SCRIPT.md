## Governing claim

Humanity's dignity is received, not earned and not self-assigned. God made mankind in His own image and likeness (Genesis 1:26-27): embodied and God-breathed, male and female, relational, upright in knowledge, righteousness, and holiness, and commissioned to fruitfulness and priestly work. The image was marred by the Fall but not erased (Genesis 9:6; James 3:9), and it is renewed in Christ, who is the image of the invisible God (Colossians 1:15), until believers bear the image of the man of heaven (1 Corinthians 15:49). Doctrinal anchor: foundational treatise Part III (Creation), sec. 5.3, "The image and the relational constitution," with sec. 5.1-5.2 and axiom III.5, at jdlongmire/oddxian-apologetics commit f7ce67904f2bc33d119dbaeb35d63c328cc9d5cf. Confessional anchor: Westminster Confession of Faith 4.2, received in substance per treatise study 01.
