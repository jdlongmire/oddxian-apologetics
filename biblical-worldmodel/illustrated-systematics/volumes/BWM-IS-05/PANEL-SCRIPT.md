## Governing claim

Creation is very good, not by accident and not yet in its final glory. It is good because God made it, ordered it, and delights in it. The volume's lexical anchor is the treatise Phase 1 study of Genesis 1:31: tov means fitting, excellent of its kind, approved by God; meod intensifies degree only; the completion vocabulary (tamim, shalem) is not used of creation in Genesis 1-2.
