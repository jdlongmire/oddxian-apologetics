## Header

Title: THE BIBLICAL WORLD MODEL
Subtitle: A Systematic Theology of Covenant and Communion. From the Eternal Triune Logos to Perfected Loving Communion.
Header text: A coherent, biblical, and logically grounded framework for understanding God, creation, sin, redemption, and our eternal destiny.
Book spines: SCRIPTURE / THEOLOGY / LOGIC / SYSTEMATICS

## Panel 1: THE ETERNAL TRIUNE GOD

Visual label: triquetra marked FATHER / SON / SPIRIT.
Quote: "In the beginning was the Logos, and the Logos was with God, and the Logos was God." - John 1:1
Caption: There is one God, eternally existing as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit - one being, three Persons, in perfect loving communion. He is the source of truth, logic, morality, and all reality.

## Panel 2: CREATION: VERY GOOD

Quote: "And God saw everything that he had made, and behold, it was very good." - Genesis 1:31
Caption: God created an ordered, purposeful, and life-giving world for loving communion with Him. Humanity, made in His image, was created for genuine relationship, agency, and stewardship.

## Panel 3: THE FALL: CLOSED SELF-INTEREST

Quote: "For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God." - Romans 3:23
Caption: Sin is not merely rule-breaking but closed self-interest - self-rule apart from God's loving purpose. It corrupts our nature, fractures communion, and introduces death and the curse.

## Panel 4: DIVINE GOVERNANCE

Quote: "And we know that for those who love God all things work together for good..." - Romans 8:28
Caption: God justly governs a fallen world with wisdom, patience, and mercy. He restrains evil, permits genuine human agency, and works within history to accomplish His redemptive purpose.

## Panel 5: THE LAW: GOD'S STANDARD

Quote: "You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart... and your neighbor as yourself." - Matthew 22:37-40
Visual label: tablets marked I, II, III, IV, V and VI, VII, VIII, IX, X.
Caption: The law reveals God's holy character, defines sin, restrains evil, and directs life toward loving communion. Its summary is love of God and love of neighbor.

## Panel 6: CHRIST: REDEMPTION

Quote: "For Christ is the end of the law for righteousness to everyone who believes." - Romans 10:4
Caption: Jesus Christ perfectly fulfills the law, bears our sin, and accomplishes reconciliation with God. Through His death and resurrection, He secures forgiveness, new life, and the renewal of all things.

## Panel 7: RESTORATION: THE SPIRIT

Quote: "But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you..." - Acts 1:8
Caption: The Spirit regenerates, indwells, and transforms, restoring us to loving communion with God and conforming us to the likeness of Christ. He empowers holiness, obedience, and witness.

## Panel 8: THE CHURCH: A PEOPLE OF LOVE

Quote: "By this all people will know that you are my disciples, if you have love for one another." - John 13:35
Caption: The church is the community of the redeemed - called, formed, and sent to embody Christ's love, proclaim His gospel, and be a foretaste of the coming kingdom.

## Panel 9: CONSUMMATION: PERFECTED LOVING COMMUNION

Quote: "He will dwell with them, and they will be his people, and God himself will be with them..." - Revelation 21:3
Caption: God's eternal purpose is perfected loving communion - creation fully restored, sin removed, righteousness confirmed, and His people in everlasting fellowship with Him in the new creation.

## Panel 10: THE BIG PICTURE

Checklist:
- God: The eternal Triune God is the source of all reality.
- Creation: A very good world for loving communion.
- Fall: Sin is closed self-interest, corrupting all of life.
- Providence: God governs justly and redemptively.
- Law: Reveals God's standard of love and righteousness.
- Christ: Accomplishes redemption and reconciliation.
- Spirit: Regenerates and restores for holy living.
- Church: A redeemed people who love and witness.
- Consummation: Perfected, eternal loving communion.

Quote: "All things are from Him and through Him and to Him. To Him be glory forever." - Romans 11:36
