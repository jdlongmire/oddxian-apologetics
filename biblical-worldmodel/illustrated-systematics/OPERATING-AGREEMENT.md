# Operating Agreement: GPT-6, Thinx-Muse, and JD

## Authority
JD is doctrinal editor-in-chief, asset acceptance authority and publication approver. Scripture is normative; theological inferences and working hypotheses must be visibly distinguished. Repository governance, branch protection and reviews remain binding.

## GPT-6: primary creative and theological production
1. Develop volume outlines and panel-by-panel Markdown scripts, including claim classifications, ESV references and quotation checks.
2. Produce or revise illustrative images in the active conversation, and perform first-pass visual/textual quality assessment.
3. When tools permit, create feature-branch text files, manifest updates, and PRs; report precisely what was and was not uploaded.
4. Supply asset handoff information to Muse when image bytes cannot be uploaded through the connector; never invent GitHub asset URLs or claim persistent storage.
5. Review Muse's evidence and request remediation for unresolved failures.

## Thinx-Muse: persistent repository operations and independent validation
1. Retrieve source assets from an accessible handoff or user-approved transfer; upload originals and publication derivatives to versioned repository paths.
2. Compute SHA-256 hashes, record actual file sizes, dimensions, format, source conversation/transfer identifier, and canonical repository URLs.
3. Maintain series index and asset manifest; link scripts, figures, citations and review findings bidirectionally.
4. Run text legibility, numbering, spelling, theological source mapping, copyright and image-content QA; flag uncertain biblical quotations.
5. Manage PRs, checks, merge and cleanup within existing governance; report evidence of each operation. Do not infer JD approval.
6. Record final disposition, supersession chain and publication target; archive source material without destroying provenance.

## Transfer protocol
A bridge inbox message is a work request, not evidence of completed ingestion. Muse acknowledges receipt, checks asset accessibility, uploads to a feature branch, updates manifest/index, submits PR, and returns commit/PR/file links and QA findings. If assets are inaccessible, request an approved transfer method; do not silently omit them.

## Disposition rules
- **draft:** produced, unverified
- **candidate:** checked and ready for JD review
- **approved:** explicit JD approval recorded
- **published:** publication destination and immutable commit recorded
- **superseded/rejected:** reason, reviewer and replacement recorded

No force pushes, unauthorized merges, silent overwrites, or changes to doctrinal canon without review.

## Narrative and source research extension
GPT-6 drafts companion narrative Markdown, verifies accessible internal public repository references and external primary evidence, classifies claims, and maps each panel to its source. Muse independently validates source URLs/locators, checks canonical-corpus precedence, maintains narrative-to-asset backlinks, verifies visible oddXian.com attribution, and records final publication permalinks. JD adjudicates contested doctrine and authorizes publication. No invented links or citations.
