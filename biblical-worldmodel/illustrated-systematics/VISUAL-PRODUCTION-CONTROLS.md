# Visual Production and Character Continuity Controls

**Authority:** supplement to the existing [Visual Series Style Guide](style-guide.jpg), which remains the governing visual exemplar. **Scope:** BWM-IS plates and publication derivatives.

## Character identity
Use JD's approved illustrated exemplar. Preserve bald head, amber glasses, white goatee, clean-shaven upper lip, facial proportions and professional, approachable scholarly appearance. The same person must remain recognizable in all volumes. Do not replace with generic stock-like figures. Record the approved exemplar as an asset with provenance.

## Deliberate variety
Rotate shirts among red, purple, blue, navy, green, burgundy, slate and charcoal. Match jackets and settings to subject matter. Vary poses: teaching, reading Scripture, thinking, researching, writing, dialogue, analyzing, presenting, questioning, summarizing and encouraging. Avoid repetitive outfit/pose combinations in adjacent volumes. Variations must preserve identity and consistent comic-illustrative style.

## Layout and theology
Maintain the ten-panel format, panel numbering, coherent navy/gold/parchment series identity, legible labels, and **oddXian.com** attribution. Publication derivatives should carry **BWM-IS-XX**. Do not anthropomorphically depict divine Persons. Symbols require contextual doctrinal review; a dove is not automatic approval to depict the Spirit.

## Image QA and revision control
1. Generate from approved panel script and narrative; verify all visible text against both.
2. Inspect every title, small quotation box, Scripture citation, panel number, checklist and footer for garbling, truncation, omissions or fabricated wording.
3. For localized defects, regenerate or correct and recheck the entire plate, since image editing can change unrelated text.
4. Compare face, upper lip, glasses, pose and shirt palette to the exemplar.
5. Preserve source and derivative versions, record hashes, image dimensions, QA outcome, supersession links, and JD's disposition.
6. No inferred publication, no invented repository asset URL, no promotion to final solely from image generation.

## Responsibilities
GPT-6 proposes visual variations, generates or edits plates, reviews first-pass consistency, prepares script/narrative changes and hands off available assets. Muse checks actual asset accessibility, ingests and indexes binary files, independently verifies text and visual fidelity, documents defects and revisions, manages governance and publication evidence. JD alone approves doctrinal and publication disposition.
